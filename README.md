### Hi 👋 I'm Yannick

I am a Beninese **engineering student** at **École Centrale de Nantes** (Aeronautic Engineering, Research & Development track), building a research and engineering profile focused on **structural analysis of aircraft structures**, with strong appetites for **numerical simulation, research, and design optimization**.


#### 🎯 What I work on

My work bridges three pillars:

- **Structural analysis (core focus)** — finite element modeling and dimensioning of aircraft structures: linear and non-linear FEM, dynamics, vibrations, composites, damage and fatigue
- **Numerical simulation & research** — advanced numerical methods, simulation validation, verification, uncertainty quantification, physics-informed machine learning
- **Design optimization** — parametric and topological optimization for lighter, safer, more durable aircraft
